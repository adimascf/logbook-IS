Eris only reported promoters that are inside the IS elements from the input EC958 chromosome.

Reading through eris codebase and investigating the output of eris from both chromosome and gfa file, it looks like that eris only search promoter motifs in the identified IS elements..

so i think eris assums if:

“If an IS has a promoter inside it, and a nearby gene lies downstream on the same strand, that gene may potentialy be upregulated.”

this paper Tempel et al 2022 found that ISs often contain internal promoters capable of initiating transcription either inward (for their transposase) or outward (toward flanking genes). Out of ~57 k regulatory motifs found in ISs,94.7% were promoters, 0.4% were terminators, and 4.9 % were transcription-factor binding sites.

They also used threshold size of 80 bp and 80% sequence identity to ensure the specificity of detection.

They noted that 26.5% of ISs were found to contain regulatory motifs, mostly promoters, and that within the ← IS → shapes the role of ISs as promoters becomes crucial, when the IS–Gene distance is less than 100 bp..
